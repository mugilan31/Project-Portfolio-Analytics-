PMO Portfolio: From Strategy to Success
PMO Portfolio: From Strategy to Success focuses on using analytics to assess portfolio performance, identify delivery risks and support better strategic decisions. Participants will work with a multi-table dataset containing project information and monthly performance snapshots, covering areas such as schedule and cost variance, milestone delivery, resource capacity, risk exposure and benefit realization. The objective is to determine which projects should be accelerated, recovered, paused or terminated.

The analysis should help tell the story of how data can reveal the patterns, risks, opportunities, and operational drivers behind better decision-making. The best submissions will connect key trends, performance changes, process behavior, and stakeholder outcomes to practical recommendations that a business audience can act on.
